What we do

We are building a generalized verification layer—mostly focused on humanoids, but designed for any robot stack. The goal is simple: know what your system can do in virtual land before you bet on physical land.

User stories as the contract

Capabilities are expressed as user stories—clear, testable behaviors your customers and operators actually care about. No vague “AI magic.” Concrete expectations.

Simulation worlds that grade

Software builds run in simulation environments matched to those stories. Each run earns a grade per story, so teams see where they pass, where they regress, and what to fix next.

Capability history, not one-off demos

Grades accumulate in a library: same robot across software versions, same software on different hardware, benchmarks against peers. You get a grade level for the whole system—and a paper trail that builds trust with partners.

We improve teams, not weights. Our first products coach the developers building the robot—tight feedback loops, reproducible runs, and honest reports. Deep model training is not the starting point; engineering discipline is.

How it works

  1. 1

    Define stories

    Align on user stories that map to real deployments—retail, inspection, logistics, or your vertical.

  2. 2

    Run & grade in simulation

    Execute software versions in simulation worlds; each story receives a grade and diagnostic context.

  3. 3

    Track & improve

    Compare runs over time, prioritize fixes, and show partners measurable progress toward real-world readiness.

  4. 4

    Graduate to physical chaos (when ready)

    Physical test facilities and public-facing venues are on the roadmap—a place for robots to experience real human chaos. That infrastructure does not exist yet; virtual proof comes first.
