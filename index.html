Example engagement

Release question

Does this controller still walk a hallway under our stated conditions after today’s software change?

Problem

A humanoid software team needs evidence for a specific behavior before a release or pilot—not a demo reel.

You provide

  • Controller or policy
  • Robot model or thin adapter
  • Deployment stories that matter

We do

  • Agree behaviors and the success bar
  • Run repeatable simulation missions
  • Preserve evidence and compare it to a versioned baseline
  • Recommend fixes, then re-run to show what changed

You take away

  • Reusable tests
  • Preserved evidence
  • A readable report
  • A comparison to the prior version

Rough duration A first engagement typically lands in a few weeks from kickoff to first report—scoped in the opening conversation.
